\section{Matrices}
A rotation matrix in 2D is:
\begin{equation}
    R(\theta) = \begin{pmatrix}
        \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\
        \sin\theta &  \cos\theta
    \end{pmatrix}
\end{equation}

\section{Summations}
The sum of the first $n$ natural numbers:
\begin{equation}
    \sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
\end{equation}
